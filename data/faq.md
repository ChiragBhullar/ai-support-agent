# Frequently asked questions

This file is the FAQ knowledge base. Every `##` heading is one entry: the heading
is the customer question and the text below it is the approved answer.

## What are your working hours?
Our support team is available Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. This
assistant answers questions 24x7, and anything it cannot solve is picked up by the
team on the next working day.

## Are you open on weekends or public holidays?
We work on Saturdays from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM IST. We are closed on Sundays and
national public holidays. You can still raise a ticket here at any time.

## What plans do you offer and how much do they cost?
We offer three plans. Starter costs Rs 499 per month for one user. Professional
costs Rs 1,499 per month for up to five users. Enterprise has custom pricing; please
ask for a quote. All prices are exclusive of 18% GST.

## Is there a free trial?
Yes. Every new account gets a 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. No card is
needed to start the trial.

## How do I upgrade or downgrade my plan?
Go to Settings > Billing > Change plan and pick the new plan. Upgrades apply
immediately. Downgrades apply from your next billing date.

## How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Your account stays active until the
end of the current billing period. Your data is kept for 30 days after that.

## Do you provide a GST invoice?
Yes. A GST invoice is emailed after every payment and can also be downloaded from
Settings > Billing > Invoices. Add your GSTIN under Settings > Billing > Tax details
before paying so it appears on the invoice.

## Which payment methods do you accept?
We accept UPI, credit and debit cards, net banking and bank transfer for annual
Enterprise plans.

## What is your refund policy?
Monthly plans are not refunded for partial months. Annual plans can be refunded on a
pro-rata basis if cancelled within 30 days of payment. Duplicate or failed-payment
deductions are always refunded after verification.

## Where is your office located?
Our office is in Ghaziabad, Uttar Pradesh, India. Visits are by appointment only.

## How can I contact the support team?
You can chat here at any time, or email the support team. For urgent issues during
working hours, ask this assistant to connect you to a human.

## Which languages do you support?
Support is currently available in English. Hindi support is planned.

## Is my data safe?
Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest and is stored in data centres in India.
We follow the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and never sell customer data.

## How many users can I add to my account?
Starter allows one user, Professional allows up to five users, and Enterprise allows
unlimited users. You can add users under Settings > Team.
