# Troubleshooting guides

Each `##` heading is one known problem; the text below it is the approved fix.

## I can't log in to my account / login not working
1. Check that you are using the email address you signed up with.
2. Use "Forgot password" on the login page to set a new password.
3. Clear your browser cache and cookies, or try a private window.
4. If your account is locked after many attempts, wait 15 minutes and try again.

## Password reset link not working or not received
1. Check your spam or promotions folder for the reset email.
2. Reset links expire after 30 minutes; request a new one if it is older.
3. Open the link in the same browser where you requested it.
4. Make sure the email address is spelled correctly.

## Not receiving the OTP / verification code
1. Wait two minutes; OTPs can be delayed by the mobile network.
2. Check that your registered mobile number is correct under Settings > Profile.
3. Make sure DND or SMS blocking is not turned on for business messages.
4. Tap "Resend OTP". You can request up to three OTPs in 10 minutes.

## App crashes or closes when uploading a file
1. Check the file is under 25 MB and is a PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PNG or JPG.
2. Update the app to the latest version from the app store.
3. Restart your phone and try the upload again.
4. If it still crashes, try uploading from the website instead.

## App or website is slow or not loading
1. Check your internet connection by opening another website.
2. Refresh the page or restart the app.
3. Clear the browser cache or the app cache under phone settings.
4. Check our status page; there may be planned maintenance.

## Not receiving email notifications
1. Check your spam folder and mark our emails as "Not spam".
2. Go to Settings > Notifications and make sure email alerts are on.
3. Add our support email address to your contacts.

## Data not syncing between phone and website
1. Make sure you are logged in to the same account on both.
2. Pull down to refresh in the app.
3. Log out and log back in on the phone.
4. Check that the app has background data permission.

## Unable to export a report to Excel or PDF
1. Reports with more than 50,000 rows must be exported in parts using date filters.
2. Allow pop-ups for our website in your browser.
3. Try a different browser such as Chrome or Edge.

## Error message when installing the app
1. Make sure your phone has at least 200 MB of free storage.
2. The app needs Android 9+ or iOS 15+.
3. Restart the phone and install again from the official app store.
